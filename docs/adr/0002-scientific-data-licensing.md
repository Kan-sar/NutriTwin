# ADR 0002: Do not bundle restricted ICMR tables

Status: Accepted — 2026-08-30

Context: ICMR-NIN is authoritative, while full RDA/IFCT material is sold and the 2024 guidelines restrict electronic product reproduction without permission. Decision: provide a checksum-driven local import path and conspicuously synthetic demo fixtures; never label those fixtures official. USDA FDC CC0 may supply attributed demo/gap food values. Consequences: the prototype workflow is testable, but scientific validation remains blocked until lawful authoritative inputs and expert-verified golden cases are supplied.
